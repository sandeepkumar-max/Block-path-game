Thank you for playing BlockPath ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information. 1. Information We Collect BlockPath does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts. Gameplay Data: All game progress, statistics (such as matches won, high scores, coins, and themes), and user settings (sound effects, haptic vibration, dark mode) are stored locally on your device using local storage (Room Database / Android SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear app data. 2. Device Permissions Used BlockPath only requests minimal technical permissions strictly necessary for standard application operations: INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for checking network connectivity and standard platform operational services. VIBRATION: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during pawn movements and wall placements (which can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings). We do not access your camera, microphone, device storage, photos, or precise GPS location. 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising BlockPath does not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties. If third-party services (such as Google Play Services) are utilized for platform compatibility or future optional cloud services, their collection and processing of diagnostic data is governed by the Google Play Services Privacy Policy. 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance) BlockPath is a family-friendly board strategy game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since our game does not collect personal data from any user, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. 5. Security of Your Data Because all gameplay preferences and local data remain strictly on your physical Android device, you have complete control over your data. You can erase all app data at any time via your device’s Settings > Apps > BlockPath > Clear Data. 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". 7.
