Privacy Policy for BlockPath

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 | Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Thank you for playing BlockPath ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

Summary: BlockPath is designed as an offline-first board game. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

1. Information We Collect (Or Do Not Collect)

We believe in complete transparency and minimal data usage:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.
  • Local Gameplay Data: All your stats (games won, moves count, board themes, coins) and user preferences (dark theme, sound volume, vibration) are stored strictly locally on your Android device. This data never leaves your device and is wiped if you uninstall the app or clear cache.

2. Device Permissions Used

BlockPath only uses minimal permissions strictly required for standard Android app functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for standard network status checks and basic platform operational integrity.
  • VIBRATION: Used only to trigger tactile haptic vibrations during board moves and wall drops. You can disable this at any time in the in-game Settings.

We do not request access to sensitive features such as your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, or Internal Storage files.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app does not share, rent, or monetize your data. Any standard Android platform libraries (such as Google Play Services) that operate in the background are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

BlockPath is a family-safe strategy board game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Our application complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

5. Data Security & User Control

Because your game data is stored directly on your phone's internal storage, you have 100% control over your data. You can delete all your stored stats at any time by going to your phone's Settings > Apps > BlockPath > Storage > Clear Storage.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
